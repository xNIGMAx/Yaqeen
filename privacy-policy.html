Privacy Policy

Yaqeen iOS App  ·  Effective: June 21, 2026

Yaqeen is designed as a privacy-first app. Your data stays on your device. Anonymous analytics — Yaqeen uses Firebase Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example, that an onboarding step or reflection session was completed). These events are not linked to your identity, contain no personal data, and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See Google's privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

No accounts — you do not sign in to use Yaqeen.

No Yaqeen servers — we do not receive or store your data on any backend.

Delete everything — Settings → Privacy → Reset Yaqeen removes all local data.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Yaqeen mobile application ("Yaqeen," "we," "our") handles information when you use the app. If you have questions, contact us via the support email on the App Store listing.

Information stored on your device

Yaqeen stores the following data only on your device (using Apple's SwiftData and a secure App Group shared between the main app and its extensions). None of this data is sent to Yaqeen or to any server we operate.

Data Purpose Leaves device?
User settings Language, notifications, prayer setup, reflection options, onboarding progress, optional display name No
Prayer events When reminders were shown, when you confirmed prayer ("I prayed"), or dismissed prompts — for in-app experience only No
Reflection events Your sessions and progress through bundled content No
Content progress Which reflection or trivia items you have seen or completed No
Prayer location Coordinates to calculate prayer times locally on your device No
App shield selections Opaque Apple tokens representing apps/categories you chose to pause during prayer or reflection windows No
Dhikr counter Daily and lifetime tap counts for the widget and in-app counter No
Schedule data Your enabled prayer windows and scheduling configuration No

Family Activity (Screen Time) tokens

When you pick apps to shield, Apple provides opaque tokens — not app names readable by Yaqeen outside Apple's picker UI. Yaqeen stores those tokens locally to apply shields during your configured prayer (and optional reflection) windows. We cannot see your full app list or browsing history.

Bundled content

Quranic reflections, Adhkar, trivia, and similar content are bundled inside the app. Reading or completing them does not send usage reports to us.

Permissions and how they are used

Screen Time / Family Controls (required for prayer shields)

Used only to pause apps you select during prayer windows you configure. This is not parental controls, not monitoring of other people, and not blocking apps outside your windows. If denied, prayer shields will not work.

Location (optional — prayer times)

Used to compute prayer times on your device using your coordinates. Not used for tracking, advertising, or sending data to our servers (we have none).

Notifications (optional)

Local notifications scheduled on your device for prayer reminders, reflection nudges, and Azkar. Yaqeen does not use a push notification server.

Yaqeen does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, HealthKit, or Bluetooth.

What we do not collect

  • Email addresses or passwords (no accounts)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Personally-identifying analytics or usage events sent to third-party services
  • Crash reports sent to third-party crash reporters
  • Screen-time or app-usage reports uploaded to our servers
  • Messages, contacts, or media from other apps

App extensions and widgets

Yaqeen includes Apple-approved app extensions (Screen Time shield UI, activity monitoring, and home/lock screen widgets). These run on your device and read/write only to the same App Group storage as the main app. This sharing does not send data off your device.

Children

Yaqeen is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not operate accounts or collect data on our servers, we do not receive child data from the app. You can remove all data using Reset Yaqeen on that device.

Your choices and control

  • Change permissions: iOS Settings → Yaqeen
  • Change app selections: Re-open the in-app app picker in Settings
  • Delete all app data: Settings → Privacy → Reset Yaqeen (cannot be undone)
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes it and its local data from your device

Third parties

  • Apple Inc. — Yaqeen uses Apple frameworks (Screen Time, notifications, location, widgets). Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • TestFlight — Beta testing distribution is handled by Apple under their terms.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — Yaqeen uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example, that an onboarding step or reflection session was completed). These events are not linked to your identity, contain no personal data, and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • No other third-party data processors — We do not integrate Mixpanel, Amplitude, advertising networks, or similar services.

Security

Data is protected by your device's security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, encryption at rest provided by iOS). Yaqeen does not transmit your stored information to any backend.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the updated policy at the same URL and change the effective date. Continued use of Yaqeen after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the App Store product page for Yaqeen.
